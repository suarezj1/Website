Tesla Valuation & Sensitivity Analysis

FINANCE · VALUATION · EXCEL MODELING

A financial valuation case study forecasting Tesla's implied share price from 2026-2033 using discounted cash flow (DCF) analysis, scenario modeling, comparable companies analysis, and Goal Seek.

Project Tesla Stock Valuation
Method Financial Modeling & Valuation
Focus Implied Share Price

Note: This was an academic project. Any and all projections and valuation estimates are hypothetical and were developed for educational purposes.

Overview

This case study evaluated Tesla's value per share by forecasting future operating performance and comparing the resulting valuation with the company's observed market price.

The analysis used multiple approaches rather than relying on a single valuation estimate. A DCF model established the core valuation, scenario analysis tested alternative growth outcomes, and comparable-company valuation provided an additional market-based benchmark.

Core Question:

What assumptions would be required for a traditional valuation model to support Tesla's $404.63 market price?

Rather than treating the observed price per share (PPS) as the answer, the analysis tested the operating assumptions needed to justify that valuation.

Valuation Approach

The analysis combined DCF modeling, scenario analysis, Goal Seek, and comparable-company valuation. Revenue was forecast through 2033 under downside, base, and upside scenarios. Projected operating performance was then converted into FCF and discounted to present value (PV).

View Excel Model Logic 
                                    Revenue Forecast = Prior Year Revenue × (1 + Revenue Growth Rate)

                                    EBIT = Revenue - Cost of Revenue - R&D - SG&A

                                    FCF = EBIT × (1 - Tax Rate) + Depreciation - OpEx - Change in Net Working Capital

                                    Terminal Value = Final-Year FCF × (1 + Perpetual Growth Rate) ÷ (Discount Rate - Perpetual Growth Rate)

                                    PV = FCF ÷ (1 + Discount Rate)^Year

                                    Estimated PPS = Equity Value ÷ Shares Outstanding

Valuation Results

The estimated share price varied substantially depending on the assumptions used. Even the upside DCF and peer-based valuation remained well below Tesla's $404.63 market price.

Estimated PPS across DCF scenarios and peer valuation compared with Tesla's $404.63 market price.

Sensitivity

The valuation was particularly sensitive to operating costs. Increasing cost of revenue toward 85% of revenue reduced the estimated value to approximately $27.42 per share. Higher R&D spending also reduced the valuation, although the effect was less severe than the cost-of-revenue sensitivity.

MARKET PRICE TEST

What Would It Take to Justify $404.63?

Goal Seek was used to determine the revenue-growth assumptions required for the DCF model to reach Tesla's $404.63 market price. The model required approximately 73.28% revenue growth in 2026, followed by a gradual decline to 23.49% by 2033. This is a decrease of approximately 50 percentage points.

Conclusion

Estimated Valuation Range

$65.41–$123.88

The analysis produced a reasonable valuation range of approximately $65.41 to $123.88 per share using the base DCF and comparable-company approaches. Tesla's $404.63 market price therefore reflected expectations beyond the assumptions used in these traditional valuation models. Brand strength, innovation, investor sentiment, and expectations of future growth may help explain part of that premium.
